Where trailers actually rot

Not the deck — underneath it. Crossmembers, the tongue, the coupler area and every spot where water sits behind a weld. By the time surface rust is showing on top, the members below are usually well into it. Blasting is how you see the truth about what is left.

Deck off is the right call

On a wood-deck trailer, pulling the boards costs an hour and doubles the value of the job. Blasting a frame through deck gaps leaves the worst corrosion untouched, which means it comes back. If the deck is coming up anyway, that is the moment to do this.

Why this pays for itself

A stripped, primed and painted frame adds years and holds resale. Owners who repaint over scale end up doing it again in two seasons. One correct strip beats three cosmetic passes, and it is cheaper by the time you add up the paint.
